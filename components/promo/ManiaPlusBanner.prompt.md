The membership upsell strip in the home feed.

```jsx
<ManiaPlusBanner
  title="Mánia Plusz"
  subtitle="15% kedvezmény minden termékre"
  markSrc="assets/logos/app-icon-mania.png"
  onClick={openManiaPlus}
/>
```

Uses `--surface-brand-deep` (#0A2F3A) — one shade darker than the header teal, so it reads as an inset panel rather than a repeat of the chrome. Whole row is the tap target; the capsule is decorative.
