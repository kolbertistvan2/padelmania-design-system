Full-width teal card that routes to a feature — the Ütőválasztó entry point at the bottom of the home feed.

```jsx
<FeatureCta
  icon={<Icon name="crosshair" size={24} />}
  title="Nem tudod, melyik ütő a tiéd?"
  subtitle="5 kérdés, és megvan az ütőd"
  onClick={openRacketSelector}
/>
```

Only one per screen. The icon tile is 8% white on the teal ground with a coral glyph — that coral-on-teal pairing is the brand's attention signal.
