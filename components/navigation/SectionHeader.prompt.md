Header row that opens every rail and list section on the home screen.

```jsx
<SectionHeader title="Kategóriák" onSeeAll={openCategories} />
<SectionHeader title="Akciós termékek" badge="Akció" />
<SectionHeader title="Kiemelt termékek" />
```

Title is 17px semibold near-black; the "Összes" link is coral medium 13px with a bold caret. 16px side margins to match the rail gutter, 12px below.
