Small status label — sale flags, stock state, opening hours, membership tags.

```jsx
<Badge tone="brand" caps>Akció</Badge>
<Badge tone="success">Kipróbálható</Badge>
<Badge tone="open">Nyitva</Badge>
<Badge tone="accent">1.0-2.0</Badge>
<Badge tone="outline">Készleten</Badge>
```

`caps` is reserved for the shouty commerce flags (AKCIÓ) — everything else is sentence case. Product-image badges are absolutely positioned 8px from the corner: sale top-left, demo bottom-left.
